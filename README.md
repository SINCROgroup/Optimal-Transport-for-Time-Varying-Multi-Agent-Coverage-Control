# Semi-discrete Optimal Transport for Time-Varying Multi-Agent Coverage Control

This repository contains the supplementary materials and demonstration videos associated with the paper:  

**[1]** Italo Napolitano, Mario di Bernardo, *"Semi-discrete Optimal Transport for Time-Varying Multi-Agent Coverage Control,"* arXiv:2601.21753, 2026.  

# Repository structure  
- **`supplementary_materials.pdf`**: Supplementary materials of [1].
- **`MOVIE_2D.mp4`**: Simulation from Section 5 (Fig. 1 in [1]) with TVOT-G. Eight agents (black dots) track the time-varying target 2D distribution (colormap) which (1) has mean varying linearly and variance varing sinusoidally time; (2) splits into a bimodal distribution with constant variance and mean varying linearly in time.
- **`MOVIE_2D_TVOT_C.mp4`**: Simulation from Section 5 in [1] with TVOT-C. Eight agents (black dots) track the time-varying target 2D distribution (colormap) which (1) has mean varying linearly and variance varing sinusoidally time; (2) splits into a bimodal distribution with constant variance and mean varying linearly in time.
- **`MOVIE_2D_TVOT_D_1.mp4`**: Simulation from Section 5 in [1] with TVOT-D_1. Eight agents (black dots) track the time-varying target 2D distribution (colormap) which (1) has mean varying linearly and variance varing sinusoidally time; (2) splits into a bimodal distribution with constant variance and mean varying linearly in time.
- **`MOVIE_2D_TVOT_D_4.mp4`**: Simulation from Section 5 in [1] with TVOT-D_4. Eight agents (black dots) track the time-varying target 2D distribution (colormap) which (1) has mean varying linearly and variance varing sinusoidally time; (2) splits into a bimodal distribution with constant variance and mean varying linearly in time.
- **`MOVIE_1Dsinusoidal.mp4`**: Simulation from Section 5 (Fig. 3 in [1]). Five agents (black dots) track the time-varying target 1D monomodal distribution (red line) whose mean and variance vary sinusoidally in time.
- **`MOVIE_1Dbimodal.mp4`**: Additional simulation. Five agents (black dots) track the time-varying target 1D bimodal distribution (red line) whose mean varies linearly in time.
